# Contributing

Thank you for contributing to the Atum Protocol.

## Contributor License Agreement

A pull request from outside Atum can be merged only after its author is covered
by a signed Contributor License Agreement (CLA):

- **Individuals** sign the [Individual CLA](./cla/individual-v1.md). The CLA
  check on the pull request links to the signing page.
- **Companies** sign the [Corporate CLA](./cla/corporate-v1.md) and list the
  people allowed to contribute for them in its Schedule A. Send the signed form
  and any later Schedule A updates to legal@atumlabs.xyz.

If your employer owns rights in your contribution, you need its permission or
waiver, or a Corporate CLA that covers you.

The CLA covers contributions to Atum-managed repositories. It does not cover
independent implementations of Atum interfaces.

The CLA provides rights only in covered contributions. It does not remove
third-party license obligations or revoke existing Apache/MIT rights.

## What to include

- Disclose any third-party components in your pull request and their licenses.
  Material you cannot license under the CLA must be identified separately and
  marked "Third-Party Material - Not a Contribution".
- Do not include confidential information, credentials, keys or secrets.
